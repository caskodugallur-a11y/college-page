ACADEMIC EXCELLENCE IN KODUNGALLUR

College of Applied Science Kodungallur is a flagship institution managed by the Institute of Human Resources Development (IHRD), a Government of Kerala Undertaking. Affiliated with the University of Calicut, the college serves as a beacon of high-quality higher education in science, technology, and commerce.

"Our mission is to empower young minds through cutting-edge technical curriculum, practical industry exposure, and ethical values."
